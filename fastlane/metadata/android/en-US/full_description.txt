Mumla Funk turns Mumble into a walkie-talkie. It connects to regular Mumble servers and is made for groups that use a Mumble server as their radio network: everyone talks with push-to-talk, quickly and without looking.

* Push-to-talk popup: one big button, hold to talk, release to stop. Shows the server, the connection and who is talking. Volume-down is the talk key while it is open. Works over the lock screen.
* Hold the power button to open the popup (set Mumla Funk as digital assistant app).
* Hold volume-down for 2 seconds to open the popup; a short press still changes the volume.
* Two home screen widgets, each bound to a server: a small talk button and a large widget with everyone online. A crossed-out radio tower shows when you are not connected.
* Simple home screen with server, status, talk button and user list.
* Reliable end of transmission: the end-of-transmission flag is always sent when you release the button, like the desktop Mumble client.
* Everything from Mumla stays available: channels, chat, certificates, access tokens, Tor.

Mumla Funk is a fork of Mumla and can be installed next to it. It is copylefted libre software (GPLv3): https://github.com/Filubu/mumla-funk
